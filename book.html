About Fertility Treatment

Fertility treatment involves medical procedures to help individuals or couples conceive. Techniques such as In Vitro Fertilization (IVF), Intrauterine Insemination (IUI), and Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI) are used to overcome fertility challenges.

Our clinic focuses on patient-centered care, ensuring emotional support and advanced reproductive technology to maximize success rates.

Techniques Offered

  • IVF: Combining eggs and sperm outside the body and implanting embryos.
  • IUI: Placing prepared sperm directly into the uterus.
  • ICSI: Direct injection of a single sperm into an egg to increase fertilization chances.

Ideal Candidates

  • Couples with unexplained infertility.
  • Women with blocked fallopian tubes or ovulation issues.
  • Men with low sperm count or motility issues.
  • Individuals seeking assisted conception options.
